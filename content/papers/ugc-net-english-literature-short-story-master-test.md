## UGC NET English Literature

## SHORT STORY MASTER TEST – 75 MCQs

Based on 20 Years of Exam Patterns • 2 Marks Each • Total: 150 Marks

Instructions: Choose the most appropriate answer for each question.

**1.** Which short story by Edgar Allan Poe ends with the line “In pace requiescat!” after a man is walled up alive?
    A) The Tell-Tale Heart
    B) The Cask of Amontillado
    C) The Pit and the Pendulum
    D) The Black Cat

**2.** In James Joyce’s “The Dead,” the song that triggers Gretta Conroy’s memory of her lost love is:
    A) “The Lass of Aughrim”
    B) “The Croppy Boy”
    C) “She Moved Through the Fair”
    D) “Danny Boy”

**3.** Akaky Akakievich, the protagonist of Nikolai Gogol’s “The Overcoat,” works as a:
    A) Court clerk
    B) Copyist
    C) Schoolteacher
    D) Government minister

**4.** What is the name of the young girl in Saki’s “The Open Window” who fabricates a tale about the lost hunters?
    A) Vera
    B) Mabel
    C) Sappleton
    D) Agnes

**5.** In D.H. Lawrence’s “The Rocking-Horse Winner,” the phrase the house seems to whisper is:
    A) “There must be more money”
    B) “Luck is everything”
    C) “The horse knows”
    D) “Win or lose”

**6.** Jhumpa Lahiri’s “A Temporary Matter” revolves around a couple revealing secrets during:
    A) A snowstorm
    B) A scheduled power outage
    C) A weekend getaway
    D) A dinner party

**7.** Which of the following is NOT a short story collection by Anton Chekhov?
    A) The Lady with the Dog and Other Stories
    B) The Darling and Other Stories
    C) The Death of Ivan Ilyich and Other Stories
    D) Ward No. 6 and Other Stories
